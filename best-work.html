Book-Rook

This project is called Book Rook,this was the first offical/big project I had done in this class.The reason why I first chose this app was because I believe it is one of the best represenations of my best works. As I have mentioned before, I went into this class with no experience at all in coding or the process of even making an app but, I was able to do this. This shows all that I had learned throughout that portion of the unit and overall I am very proud of it. The most difficult part was adding every part of the rubric and figuring out a way to connect all the pages so that there could be a home page anyone could go back to. For this project I learned the skills of trust the process and creativity; the focus was applying all I knew.

Speedy-Man
