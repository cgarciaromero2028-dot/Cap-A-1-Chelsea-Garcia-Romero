Disney-Princess Recommendation App

This project is called Disney-Princess Recommendation app and it is the project that I felt truly showed my growth within this class. This project was my first final for Intro to Computer Science; all that I had learned from that semester was put into this last project for the section. I believe this shows my growth within this class because I applied all my skills into this app and used what I learned. Over this course, I learned the whole process in designing and learning from my mistakes. What was challenging about this projecting was incoporating stats and figuring out how many points equaled a specfic princess. The way I overcame this was remebering the lessons and try my best to do the best I could. All the work I had done for this class had helped me in this moment and had prepared me to have a sucessful app. If I could I would like to change how I handled the stats portion, I felt some answers didn't align with the princess. Overall, this app helped me improve and truly represents how much I've grown.
